# Design Notes — Midterm Exam

**Coverage:** Naive Bayes classification by hand, neural network forward pass + MSE loss + one step of gradient descent by hand, CNN parameter/output-shape calculations, precision/recall/accuracy trade-offs in a medical diagnosis context, and manual logistic regression inference.

**Goal:** Test whether students can compute these methods by hand on small examples, not just call library functions — this catches gaps that pure coding assignments can hide.

**⚠️ Before publishing:** Check whether this exact exam (or a close variant) will be reused in a future term. If so, consider:
- Sharing only 1–2 sample questions instead of the full exam, or
- Waiting until the course has moved on to different material, or
- Getting a quick go-ahead from the instructor

Publishing exam content that's still in active rotation risks giving future students an unfair advantage and can be an academic-integrity issue for the course, even though you wrote it yourself.
